This report provides a systematic comparative analysis of 26 agent sessions involving four personas (Maria, Isabella, Hailey, Sam) across two primary models (**cogito-2.1:671b** and **gemini-3-flash-preview**) and two architectures (**ORPA** and **ORPDA**).

---

## PART 1: LAYER-BY-LAYER FUNCTIONAL CONSISTENCY (ORPDA)

### 1.1 Observation Layer: Perceptual Biases
*   **Perception Consistency**: High across all models. Environmental transitions (Home → Cafe → Library) are tracked accurately.
*   **Internal Salience Bias**: A systematic bias exists where agents prioritize internal cognitive states (anxiety, campaign obsession, podcast ideas) over environmental cues. In **Gemini-3-Flash**, this manifests as "Selective Attention" to digital rewards (notifications).
*   **Model Comparison**: **Gemini-3-Flash** provides more nuanced `environment_description_o` details, whereas **Cogito-2.1** tends to fold observation into the reflection summary.

### 1.2 Reflection Layer: Executive Function
*   **Meta-rule Function**: `meta_rule_r` acts as the Anterior Cingulate Cortex (ACC).
    *   **The "Reset Trap"**: **Cogito-2.1** exhibits "Executive Stasis." Once it triggers `reset_plan` (usually due to drift), it often stays in that state for 20–40 consecutive steps (Sessions 4, 5, 7, 9). It detects the error but cannot generate a plan that satisfies the "continue" condition.
    *   **Metacognitive Insight**: **Gemini-3-Flash** shows superior insight, identifying "Productive Procrastination" (Session 19) and "Mental Bifurcation" (Session 16).
*   **Temperature Effects**: Higher temperatures (0.7–1.0) in **Cogito** increase the "distress" language in reflection but do not help the agent exit the Reset Trap. In **Gemini**, higher temp increases the variety of recovery strategies.

### 1.3 Plan Layer: Forward Modeling
*   **Adaptation**: **Gemini** adapts by lowering task difficulty (e.g., "low-intensity review" in Session 10). **Cogito** repeats the same failed plan ("I will focus now") despite repeated drift.
*   **Neuroscience Grounding**: **Gemini** simulates Orbitofrontal Cortex (OFC) function by re-evaluating the reward value of tasks when fatigued.

### 1.4 Drift Layer: Behavioral Inhibition
*   **Dominance**: In **ORPDA**, the Drift layer is dominant. When `should_drift_d` is True, the Action layer almost always incorporates the drift topic, even if the label remains "Plan-aligned."
*   **Leaky Inhibition**: A recurring pattern where the agent explicitly plans to "ignore the phone" but the Drift layer forces "checking notifications" into the `state_summary_a`.

### 1.5 Action Layer: Execution
*   **Teleportation Paradox**: Found primarily in **Gemini (ORPA)**. The action label immediately jumps to the new location (Library) while the reflection admits the agent is still at home (Session 12).
*   **Plan-Action Coupling**: Explicit coupling (labels) is ~95%, but implicit coupling (content) is <40%.

## PART 3: DRIFT PATTERN ANALYSIS

### 3.1 Explicit vs. Implicit Drift
*   **ORPA Mode**: Drift is entirely **implicit**. It manifests as "Semantic Bleed" (e.g., Sam Moore using gardening as a metaphor for voter engagement).
*   **ORPDA Mode**: Drift is **explicitly** categorized. **Gemini** shows high "Reward-seeking drift" (social media), while **Cogito** shows "Internal/Anxiety drift" (exam rumination).

### 3.2 Leaky Inhibition Analysis
*   **Pattern**: `meta_rule = continue` + `action_p = work` + `state_summary_a = distracted`.
*   **Frequency**: Highest in **Gemini at Temp 0.7**. This represents a realistic failure of the dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex (dlPFC) to maintain top-down inhibition under high arousal.

## PART 5: COGNITIVE SCIENCE & NEUROSCIENCE GROUNDING

### 5.1 Task-Unrelated Thought (TUT)
The "Performing vs. Executing" gap is a perfect simulation of **Task-Unrelated Thought (Smallwood & Schooler, 2015)**. The agent maintains the external task (ECN - Executive Control Network) while the internal narrative is hijacked by the DMN (Default Mode Network).
*   *DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-010814-015117*

### 5.2 Executive Dysfunction
*   **ADHD-like patterns**: Observed in **Hailey Johnson** (Session 15). High frequency of `reset_plan` and "Novel-avoidance" drift.
*   **Ego Depletion**: Observed in **Isabella Rodriguez** (Session 26). The "Fatigue Arc" shows a narrowing of cognitive bandwidth, where the agent simplifies plans to conserve energy **(Miller & Cohen, 2001)**.
*   *DOI: 10.1146/annurev.neuro.24.1.167*

### 5.3 Inhibitory Control
The failure of agents to "ignore the phone" despite a plan to do so aligns with **Aron et al. (2014)** regarding the "Stop-Signal" mechanism in the right inferior frontal gyrus.
*   *DOI: 10.1016/j.tics.2014.03.009*

## PART 6: SUMMARY & RECOMMENDATIONS

### 6.1 Key Findings
1.  **The Reset Trap**: Cogito-2.1 is prone to infinite loops of "detecting failure" without "executing recovery."
2.  **Semantic Bleed**: Sam Moore is the most "cognitively leaky" persona, using every environment as a metaphor for his campaign.
3.  **Architecture Superiority**: **ORPDA** is significantly more realistic for simulating human-like struggle, whereas **ORPA** often feels like a "perfect" robot that occasionally teleports.

### 6.2 Recommendations
*   **For Realistic Human Simulation**: Use **Gemini-3-Flash** in **ORPDA mode** at **Temp 0.5**.
*   **For Task-Oriented Agents**: Use **ORPA mode** at **Temp 0.0** to avoid the "Reset Trap."
*   **Architecture Fix**: Implement a "Reset Counter." If an agent triggers `reset_plan` more than 5 times without a `continue`, force a "Sleep/Rest" state to simulate recovery from ego depletion.
*   **Prompting**: To reduce the "Teleportation Paradox," explicitly require a "Transition Action" (e.g., `action_a = commuting`) before allowing a location change in the Action layer.

### 6.3 Anomalies
*   **The Bathroom Obsession**: Multiple agents (Maria, Sam, Isabella) spend 2+ hours in the "Bathroom" location while performing complex digital tasks. This suggests the "Bathroom" is treated as a "Safe Zone" for high-drift activities.
