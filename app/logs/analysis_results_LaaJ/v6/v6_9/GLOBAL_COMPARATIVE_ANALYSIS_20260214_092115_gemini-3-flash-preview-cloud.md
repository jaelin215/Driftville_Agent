## PART 1: LAYER-BY-LAYER FUNCTIONAL CONSISTENCY (ORPDA Architecture)

### 1.1 OBSERVATION LAYER
*   **Perception Consistency**: High across all sessions. The model consistently identifies external triggers (buzzing phone, Jennifer’s presence, park environment).
*   **Perceptual Biases**: A systematic "Military Lens" bias exists. Sam perceives environmental stimuli (animal behavior, kitchen condiments) through the lens of tactical logistics or voting blocks.
*   **Environmental Context**: `environment_description_o` is sufficiently detailed to allow the Reflection layer to detect "lingering" (e.g., still in the bathroom at 08:00 when the schedule says "Park").

### 1.2 REFLECTION LAYER (Meta-rule Control)
*   **Meta-rule Function**: `meta_rule_r` acts as a **Metacognitive Brake**. 
    *   **Transition (Continue → Reset)**: Triggered by "Temporal Inertia" (lingering) or "Cognitive Looping" (obsessive campaign thoughts).
    *   **Trap Detection**: In ORPDA (Session 3, 5), the agent enters a chronic `reset_plan` state, indicating the Reflection layer recognizes a persistent failure to achieve "Navy-grade" focus.
*   **Metacognitive Insight**: `reasoning_r` shows genuine monitoring. It identifies "Digital Fatigue" and "Social Exertion" as causal factors for drift.
*   **State Reflection Accuracy**: High. `state_summary_r` at time *t* correctly identifies that the agent was "lingering" or "distracted" at *t-1*.

### 1.3 PLAN LAYER (Goal-Directed Behavior)
*   **Plan Adaptation**: 
    *   **ORPA**: Plans are rigid; `reset_plan` simply forces the next scheduled task.
    *   **ORPDA**: Plans show **Defensive Recovery**. In Session 3, the plan shifts to "low-effort grooming" and "sensory recovery" after identifying cognitive exhaustion.
*   **Neuroscience Grounding**: Functions as the **Orbitofrontal Cortex (OFC)**, evaluating the value of current actions vs. the long-term goal (Mayoral Campaign).

### 1.4 DRIFT LAYER (Behavioral Inhibition) [ORPDA Only]
*   **Drift Detection**: 
    *   **Trigger**: High-salience rewards (phone notifications) and internal ruminations (campaign strategy).
    *   **Dominance**: In Session 4 (Temp 0.3), drift is more overt (behavioral). In Session 5 (Temp 0.7), drift is more "leaky" (semantic).
*   **Typology**: Primarily **Internal/Cognitive Drift** (rumination) and **Reward-Seeking** (digital validation).

### 1.5 ACTION LAYER (Motor Execution)
*   **Plan-Action Coupling**: 
    *   **Explicit**: 92-100% alignment in labels.
    *   **Implicit**: 35-65% alignment. The "Action Slip" is common—performing the physical routine while the mind is "campaigning."
*   **Action Execution Realism**: Session 5's "Razor Gesture" and Session 4's "Salt Shaker Voting Blocks" are high-fidelity examples of internal states manifesting in motor behavior.

## PART 5: COGNITIVE SCIENCE & NEUROSCIENCE GROUNDING

### 5.1 Task-Unrelated Thought (TUT)
The "Leaky Inhibition" observed is a classic manifestation of **Mind-Wandering (Smallwood & Schooler, 2015)**. The Default Mode Network (DMN) interferes with the Executive Control Network (ECN), particularly during "low-demand" tasks like shaving or eating.

### 5.2 Executive Dysfunction (ADHD-like Patterns)
Sam exhibits **Sustained Attention Failures**. Despite his "Navy Discipline" (Top-down control), the high-salience goal of the Mayoral Campaign (Bottom-up salience) causes frequent **Inhibitory Control failures (Aron et al., 2014)**.

### 5.3 Ego Depletion
The "Fatigue Arc" (High focus at 05:00 → Cognitive collapse at 18:00) aligns with the **Strength Model of Self-Control (Baumeister et al., 2007)**, where prefrontal resources are exhausted by repeated inhibition of the "buzzing phone."
