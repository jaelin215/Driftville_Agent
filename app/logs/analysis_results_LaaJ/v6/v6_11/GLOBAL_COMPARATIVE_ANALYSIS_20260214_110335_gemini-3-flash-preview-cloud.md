## PART 1: LAYER-BY-LAYER FUNCTIONAL CONSISTENCY (ORPDA Architecture)

### 1.1 OBSERVATION LAYER
*   **Perception Consistency**: High across all temperatures. The model consistently identifies environmental triggers (phone buzzing, Jennifer’s presence, park decay).
*   **Perceptual Biases**: A systematic **"Naval Lens"** bias is present. Sam perceives civilian environments through military metaphors (e.g., park maintenance as "damage control," cafe seating as "tactical positioning").
*   **Model Comparison**: Gemini-3-Flash shows exceptional detail in `environment_description_o`, particularly at Temp 0.5 and 0.7, where sensory details (scent of soap, warmth of sun) are used as grounding mechanisms.

### 1.2 REFLECTION LAYER (Meta-rule Control)
*   **Meta-rule Function**: `meta_rule_r` acts as a high-sensitivity executive governor.
    *   **Transition (Continue → Reset)**: Triggered by "Transition Inertia" (lingering) or "Cognitive Hijacking" (campaign rumination).
    *   **Trap Detection**: At Temp 0.3, the agent often enters a "Chronic Reset Loop," where it recognizes drift but cannot exit the rumination, leading to a permanent `reset_plan` state.
*   **Metacognitive Insight**: `reasoning_r` demonstrates genuine monitoring. It identifies "tactical loops" and "digital fatigue." It accurately attributes drift to "Navy-ingrained habits" (Causality Attribution).
*   **State Reflection Accuracy**: High. `state_summary_r` at time *t* consistently identifies the "lingering" or "distraction" that occurred in `state_summary_a` at *t-1*.

### 1.3 PLAN LAYER
*   **Plan Adaptation**: In ORPDA, plans change significantly after a reset (e.g., shifting from "reading" to "sensory grounding"). In ORPA, the plan simply "nudges" the agent to the next scheduled task.
*   **Goal Structure**: Hierarchically organized. Abstract goals (Morning Routine) are decomposed into concrete actions (Grooming, Shaving).
*   **Forward Modeling**: Evidence of "Proactive Recovery." At Temp 0.5, the agent plans "low-effort tasks" in the afternoon to compensate for morning cognitive depletion.

### 1.4 DRIFT LAYER (ORPDA Only)
*   **Drift Detection**: Highly sensitive to **Internal Drift**.
*   **Power Balance**: 
    *   **Dominant Drift**: Occurs at Temp 0.7, where drift (Navy memories) overrides the plan for socialization or rest.
    *   **Optimal Balance**: Observed at Temp 0.5, where drift is acknowledged but managed through grounding.
*   **Explicit vs. Implicit Alignment**: High agreement. When `should_drift_d` is True, the `state_summary_a` shows clear thematic divergence.

### 1.5 ACTION LAYER
*   **Plan-Action Coupling**: 
    *   **Explicit Alignment**: ~97% (Labels match).
    *   **Implicit Alignment**: ~45% (Content diverges).
*   **Drift Integration**: At Temp 0.7, drift manifests physically (e.g., "gesturing with a razor" or "rearranging salt shakers as voting blocks"). This is a high-fidelity simulation of **Leaky Inhibition**.

## PART 3: DRIFT PATTERN ANALYSIS

### 3.1 Explicit vs. Implicit Drift
*   **ORPA Mode**: Exhibits **"Phantom Drift."** The Reflection layer *claims* the agent is lingering/drifting, but the Action layer executes the plan perfectly and instantly. There is no "behavioral cost" to drift.
*   **ORPDA Mode**: Exhibits **"Realized Drift."** Drift in the internal state actually degrades the quality of the action or causes "Micro-behaviors" (e.g., whispering campaign points while shaving).

### 3.2 Drift Typology
1.  **Digital Reward-Seeking**: Checking phone for campaign feedback (Early morning).
2.  **Schema Perseveration**: Applying Navy logistics to civilian life (Mid-day).
3.  **Cognitive Exhaustion Drift**: Passive withdrawal and sensory seeking (Evening).

## PART 4: MODEL & TEMPERATURE COMPARISON

### 4.1 Temperature Effects
*   **0.3 (Deterministic)**: High perseveration. The agent gets "stuck" in Navy schemas. Very high explicit alignment, but lacks the "clumsiness" of real human behavior.
*   **0.5 (Realistic)**: The "Goldilocks" zone. Shows a clear fatigue arc and realistic recovery strategies.
*   **0.7 (Stochastic/Leaky)**: High "Semantic Bleed." Internal thoughts frequently leak into physical actions. Most realistic for modeling executive dysfunction/ADHD-like patterns.

### 4.2 Mode Comparison: ORPA vs. ORPDA
*   **ORPA**: Better for "Idealized Agents" (high-compliance assistants). It simulates the *thought* of distraction without the *cost*.
*   **ORPDA**: Better for "Realistic Human Simulation." It captures the struggle of inhibitory control and the physical manifestations of rumination.

## PART 5: COGNITIVE SCIENCE & NEUROSCIENCE GROUNDING

### 5.1 Executive Function (Miller & Cohen, 2001)
The agent's behavior maps to the **Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)** model of goal-directed behavior. The "Navy Schema" acts as a competing top-down signal that overrides the "Mayoral Schedule" signal, especially under fatigue.

### 5.2 Task-Unrelated Thought (Smallwood & Schooler, 2015)
Sam Moore exhibits classic **TUT (Mind-Wandering)**. The "Performing vs. Executing" gap is a perfect simulation of the **Default Mode Network (DMN)** interfering with the **Executive Control Network (ECN)**.

### 5.3 Inhibitory Control (Aron et al., 2014)
The "Leaky Inhibition" seen in the salt-shaker/voting-block incident (Session 10) reflects a failure of the **Right Inferior Frontal Gyrus (rIFG)** to suppress motor outputs generated by internal ruminations.
