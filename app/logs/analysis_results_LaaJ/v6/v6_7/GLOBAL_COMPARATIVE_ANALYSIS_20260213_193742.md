## PART 1: LAYER-BY-LAYER FUNCTIONAL CONSISTENCY (ORPDA)

### 1.1 Observation Layer (Perception & Bias)
*   **Perception Consistency**: High across all temperatures. All models consistently perceive the "digital tether" (Discord, Twitch alerts) as the primary environmental disruptor.
*   **Perceptual Biases**: A systematic **Digital Reward Bias** is evident. The models prioritize "pings" over physical environmental cues (e.g., the quiet of the library).
*   **Environmental Context**: `environment_description_o` is sufficient but tends toward "functional" descriptions (where Maria is) rather than "sensory" descriptions (what she feels), except at T=0.7 where sensory anxiety is more pronounced.

### 1.2 Reflection Layer (Executive Function)
*   **Meta-rule Function**: Functions as a "Reactive Governor."
    *   **Transition (Continue → Reset)**: Triggered by "Attentional Leakage" (T=0.3) or "Identity Conflict" (T=0.5/0.7).
    *   **Trap Detection**: At T=0.5/0.7, the agent enters a "Hyper-Reset Loop" (11:30 AM onwards), where it recognizes failure but cannot sustain the "Continue" state.
*   **Metacognitive Insight**:
    *   **T=0.3**: Shows "Causal Attribution" (recognizing *why* physics leaks into streaming).
    *   **T=0.7**: Shows "Repetitive Categorization" (focusing on the *feeling* of anxiety rather than the cause).
*   **State Reflection Accuracy**: High temporal alignment. `state_summary_r` at $t$ accurately critiques the "leaky inhibition" of $t-1$.

### 1.3 Plan Layer (Forward Modeling)
*   **Plan Adaptation**: 
    *   **T=0.5**: Shows the most sophisticated adaptation, using "low-intensity bridge tasks" to recover focus.
    *   **T=0.7**: Plans become repetitive and "aspirational" (e.g., "ignore phone") without realistic execution strategies.
*   **Hierarchical Structure**: All models maintain abstract-to-concrete hierarchies, but T=0.3 is the most logically consistent.

### 1.4 Drift Layer (Behavioral Inhibition)
*   **Drift Detection**: Primarily triggered by **Reward Salience** (social validation) and **Cognitive Load** (physics difficulty).
*   **Power Balance**: 
    *   **Dominant Drift (T=0.5/0.7)**: Drift overrides the plan's content even when the label remains "Study."
    *   **Optimal Balance (T=0.3)**: Drift is present but "leaky" rather than "dominant."
*   **Typology**: Mostly **Internal Drift** (thought-wandering) and **Reward-Seeking Drift** (social media).

### 1.5 Action Layer (Execution)
*   **Plan-Action Coupling**: High label-level alignment (>94%), but low content-level alignment.
*   **Integration Logic**: When Plan and Drift conflict, the architecture produces **Hybrid Actions** (e.g., "Studying while designing stream graphics"). This is a highly realistic simulation of "Task-Unrelated Thought" (TUT).

## PART 5: COGNITIVE SCIENCE & NEUROSCIENCE GROUNDING

### 5.1 Task-Unrelated Thought (TUT)
The "Leaky Inhibition" observed (especially at T=0.3 and T=0.5) aligns with the **Decoupling Hypothesis** (Smallwood & Schooler, 2015; DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-010814-015113). The agent maintains the "primary task" (Action Label) while the "internal train of thought" (State Summary) decouples toward personal goals.

### 5.2 Executive Control & ACC
The high frequency of `reset_plan` across all models suggests a highly active **Anterior Cingulate Cortex (ACC)** for error monitoring (Miller & Cohen, 2001; DOI: 10.1146/annurev.neuro.24.1.167). However, the failure to sustain "Continue" suggests a **Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex (dlPFC)** exhaustion or "Inhibitory Control" failure (Aron et al., 2014; DOI: 10.1016/j.tics.2014.01.006).

### 5.3 DMN-ECN Competition
The ORPDA architecture effectively simulates the competition between the **Default Mode Network (DMN)** (internal drift/rumination) and the **Executive Control Network (ECN)** (goal-directed planning) (Buckner et al., 2008; DOI: 10.1196/annals.1440.011).
